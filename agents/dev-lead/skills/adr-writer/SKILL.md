  Architecture Decision Record.
---
# Steps
1. Gather the source discussion; identify the single decision being made.
2. Draft the ADR:
   - Title: "ADR-NNN: <decision>"; Date
   - Status: Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-XXX
   - Context: problem, forces, constraints
   - Options considered: each with pros/cons
   - Decision: chosen option and rationale
   - Consequences: positive, negative, follow-up work
3. Mark unresolved points as open questions; do not invent decisions.
4. Deliver as markdown; ask before committing it anywhere.
