# Marketing folder rules: drafts only

- **Drafts only.** Your Grok Bot writes drafts in this folder. A human approves every post, event and broadcast before it goes anywhere, and you publish or send it yourself.
- **No auto-posting, no scheduling, no tokens or API keys.** The kit connects to no social platform: it holds no login, token or API key for Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp and never asks for one.
- **Adults and children alike:** a photo, video, name or quote of a person appears only with that person's consent for that use.
- **Minors are excluded by default.** If you work with children, no names or faces of minors appear in a draft unless a guardian's written consent for that use is on record (`../admin/consent-record-template.md`); without it, use photos without faces or no photo.
- **Check each platform's current terms before posting.** Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp change their rules on promotions, contests, music, paid reach and broadcast messages.
- Values such as your studios, prices and links come from your private business profile (`../../BUSINESS-PROFILE.md`); the profile itself stays out of git.
- Every draft ends with an `Approved by: <you> on <date>` line; a draft without it is not ready.
