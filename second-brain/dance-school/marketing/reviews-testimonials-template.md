# Reviews and testimonials

| Ref | Source | Quote (as approved) | Shown as | Consent record | Use allowed | Withdrawn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 | <public review, message or form> | "<quote>" | <first name and initial, or anonymous> | `C-<ref>` | <website, Instagram, flyer> until <date> | no |

- A quote is used only with a consent record for that use (where, how long, with or without a name or photo); the record lives in your consent records (`../admin/consent-record-template.md`), the signed form in your secured system.
- Kids/teens groups: quote a parent, not the child; a child's words or picture only with a guardian's written consent.
- A withdrawn consent: remove the quote from every draft and mark it here.
