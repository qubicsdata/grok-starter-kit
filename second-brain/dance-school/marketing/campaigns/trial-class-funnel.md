# Trial-class funnel with follow-up

Counts only, never names: the people in the funnel live in your booking, roster or messaging system.

| Stage | What happens | Owner (role) | Count this term |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Interest | a message, comment or website form asks about a group | <role> | <n> |
| 2 Trial booked | a day and group are agreed | <role> | <n> |
| 3 Trial attended | the person came to the class | Instructor 1 | <n> |
| 4 Follow-up sent | the messages below | <role> | <n> |
| 5 Pass bought | single, 4-session or 8-session pass | <role> | <n> |
| 6 Still dancing after 4 weeks | retention of the new group | <role> | <n> |

## Follow-up messages (drafts; you send them yourself)
- **Same day, after the trial:** thanks for coming; the next class is <day, hh:mm> at {{studio.1.name}}.
- **Day 3:** pass options ({{price.currency}}): single {{price.single}}, 4-session pass {{price.pass_4}}, 8-session pass {{price.pass_8}}; employer-benefit platforms we accept: {{price.benefit_platforms}}.
- **Day 7, only if there was no reply:** one friendly reminder with {{school.website}}; then stop.
- **Kids/teens trial:** the follow-up goes to the parent or guardian, written from `../../comms/parent-update-template.md`, never to the child.

## Rules
- Anyone who asks you to stop gets no further message.
- A follow-up is a draft until you approve it; nothing is sent or scheduled from the second brain.
