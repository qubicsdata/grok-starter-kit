# Consent records (references only)

A separate consent record per person and per use. Names, signed forms and contact details stay in your separate, secured roster system; this note keeps references only.

| Ref | Roster reference | Adult or minor | Consent for | Where | Given by | Written | Date | Until | Withdrawn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-<ref> | <roster ref> | adult | photo with first name | Instagram, Facebook | the person | yes | <date> | <date> | no |
| C-<ref> | <roster ref> | minor | team photo, no name | Facebook | guardian | yes | <date> | end of season | no |

## Rules
- Adults and children: no photo, video, name or quote without consent for that use.
- If you work with children, a minor's face or name is never used without a guardian's written consent; the default is no names and no faces of minors.
- Consent can be withdrawn at any time: mark it withdrawn here and remove the item from every draft.
- Keep this record separate from class lists and from marketing notes.
