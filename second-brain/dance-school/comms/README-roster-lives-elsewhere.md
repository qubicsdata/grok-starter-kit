# Comms folder rules

- This folder holds **templates only**: newsletters, reminders, replies.
- Parent communications (kids/teens groups) and adult communications are **kept separate**: `parent-update-template.md` for parents and guardians, `adult-group-update-template.md` for adult groups. Never one message or mailing list for both.
- Adults and children: no names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, health notes, photos or birthdays of students, parents or guardians here.
- If you work with children, write to the parent or guardian, never to the child, and use a child's photo or name only with a guardian's written consent.
- Placeholders such as `<parent-greeting>` are filled in your separate, secured roster or mailing system, never here.
- Roster location (a pointer, not data): `<name of your roster system>`.
