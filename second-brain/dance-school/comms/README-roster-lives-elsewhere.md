# Comms folder rules

- This folder holds **templates only**: newsletters, reminders, replies.
- No names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, health notes, photos or birthdays of students or parents.
- Placeholders such as `<student-first-name>` are filled in your separate, secured roster or mailing system, never here.
- Roster location (a pointer, not data): `<name of your roster system>`.
